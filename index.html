Welcome

Hey — thanks for stopping by. Upload your own SFX (up to 50, 3MB each), set emojis or photos on each button, sort them into packs, and play on a 17-button remote that stays on this device.

Tap the board title or a sound’s green display name to rename them. Use ✏️ on a button to change its look.

If you like it, consider donating — it helps keep tools like this going.

No login needed. Just a simple soundboard for your sounds.
